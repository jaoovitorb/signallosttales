Privacy Policy

Signal Lost Tales · Last updated: 5 October 2026

1. Scope

This policy covers the Signal Lost Tales publishing tool (the "Application"), a private application used by the channel owner to upload and schedule video content to accounts that the owner controls. The Application has a single user — the owner — and is not offered to the public.

2. Information the Application accesses

With the owner's explicit authorization, the Application accesses:

  • YouTube account data, through the YouTube Data API v3, limited to uploading videos, setting their metadata and thumbnails, and reading public statistics for the owner's own channel.
  • TikTok account data, limited to placing video files into the owner's own drafts for manual review and publication.

The Application does not access, collect or process data belonging to viewers, subscribers or any other person.

3. How information is used

Accessed data is used solely to publish the owner's own content to the owner's own accounts, and to record basic performance statistics for the owner's review. It is used for no other purpose.

4. Storage and retention

Authorization tokens and operational records are stored locally on the owner's personal computer. Nothing is transmitted to, or stored on, any server operated by the Application. Access can be revoked by the owner at any time through the respective platform's account settings, which immediately ends the Application's access.

5. Sharing

No data is sold, rented, shared or transferred to any third party. Data is exchanged only between the owner's computer and the official APIs of the platforms named above.

6. Google user data

The Application's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Google user data is never used for advertising, never sold, and never used to train generalized artificial intelligence or machine learning models.

7. Children

The Application is not directed to children and does not knowingly process any data relating to children.

8. Changes

This policy may be updated from time to time. The date above reflects the most recent revision.

9. Contact

Questions about this policy may be sent to joojgamepplays@hotmail.com.
